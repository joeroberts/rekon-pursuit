## How to read this ledger

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Accepted` | The decision or evidence was approved by the named owner(s); it may still require implementation evidence in a later task. |
| `Ready for review` | The artifact exists and is complete enough for its named independent review. |
| `Pending` | Required work or recorded evidence does not yet exist. |
| `Blocked` | A dependency prevents a task or milestone from opening. |
| `Deferred` | Explicitly out of the current milestone; it cannot be pulled forward without its stated gate. |
