## Residual risk and required later evidence

Moving the sheets and alert from the route-local view to `ContentView` can still change SwiftUI modal lifetime if an implementer alters a binding setter, reset, or action while extracting the view. The acceptance here relies on the plan's exact-verbatim move requirement and its future source/diff, signed-test, and attachment review; it does not substitute for those checks. Existing protected-export failure semantics still allow a failed write to leave an unusable output, as documented by the current worker; VD2-07 does not change that established contract.
